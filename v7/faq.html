Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the language of the website?

Use the हिन्दी | English buttons in the top bar. The choice is saved on your device and applies to every page.

How can I make the text bigger or change the contrast?

Select the accessibility button in the top bar, then choose A+ to enlarge text, A- to reduce it, A to return to normal, or the contrast button for high contrast.

Where can I find tender notices?

See the Tender page under Services.

Where can I find recruitment rules?

See the Recruitment Rules page under Services. You can search by post name.

Where can I find course calendars?

See the Course Calendar page under Training of NEPA.

May I reproduce material from this website?

Material may be reproduced free of charge after taking proper permission by writing to us, provided it is reproduced accurately and the source is acknowledged. See the Copyright Policy.

How do I report a problem with the website or its accessibility?

Write to us using the Feedback page. Tell us what the problem is and include your contact details.

How can I contact the Academy?

Email, telephone and address are given on the Feedback page and in the footer of every page.
