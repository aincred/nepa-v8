Web Content Review Policy (CRP)

Web Content Review Policy (CRP)

The Content Review Policy (CRP) of the North Eastern Police Academy (NEPA) website has a robust mechanism for reviewing its content through routine testing of web pages and the content.

Since this is a platform that provides information related to police training, capacity building, and institutional activities, the NEPA team is frequently in the process of updating and reviewing the information in order to provide up-to-date information to its users.

The feedback and engagement policy with the users itself provides an effective measure of review and check towards the content on the platform. Monitoring of the Website is done on a regular basis.
