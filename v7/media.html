  • Brighter Kashmir · 8 October 2026

    NEPA trainees learn civil-security coordination (external site, opens in new tab)

    Assam Rifles arranged an interaction between NEPA trainee officers and the Deputy Commissioner and Superintendent of Police of Changlang, Arunachal Pradesh, on how civil and security agencies work together. The trainees also met an Assam Rifles unit to learn about field challenges.

    Read the full report on Brighter Kashmir (external site, opens in new tab)

  • Brighter Kashmir · 6 October 2026

    Assam Rifles hosts police officers for border orientation (external site, opens in new tab)

    Forty-five state police officers from Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya and Sikkim, four Deputy Superintendents and 41 Sub-Inspectors, began a six-day programme with the Assam Rifles to see border security operations at first hand.

    Read the full report on Brighter Kashmir (external site, opens in new tab)

  • Highland Post · 22 December 2025

    68 officers from 5 states graduate from NEPA (external site, opens in new tab)

    Sixty-eight trainees from Meghalaya, Manipur, Nagaland, Tripura and Sikkim, 21 of them women, completed the 55th Basic Course at the Academy. The parade was reviewed by the Meghalaya DGP.

    Read the full report on Highland Post (external site, opens in new tab)

  • Meghalaya Monitor · 21 November 2023

    377 trainee officers pass out from NEPA (external site, opens in new tab)

    A total of 377 trainees, 101 of them women, from Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura completed the 52nd Basic Course. Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma reviewed the passing-out parade.

    Read the full report on Meghalaya Monitor (external site, opens in new tab)

  • The Morung Express (IANS) · 6 April 2023

    4 NE police training academies get Home Minister's trophy for 'best performance' (external site, opens in new tab)

    The Union Home Minister's Trophy for 2021-22 went to four north-eastern police training institutions. NEPA, Meghalaya, and the KTD Singh Police Training Academy, Tripura, won the national-level awards.

    Read the full report on The Morung Express (IANS) (external site, opens in new tab)

    Sixty-one trainees, 12 of them women, from Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, Manipur, Sikkim and Meghalaya completed the 48th Basic Course and a Post Graduate Diploma in Police Administration and Investigation. The parade went ahead in heavy rain.
