Entrance and Administration

  • R P Gate

    R P Gate is the main entrance of NEPA.

    The eight pillars of the RP Gate represent the eight states of the North East.

  • Administrative Building

    The Administrative Building is the focal point of the Academy, where all the administrative matters are initiated.

  • Academy House

    The Academy House is the official residence of the Director.

    It is centrally located amid greenery.

Training Facilities

  • Indoor Training Block

    The Academy has a state of the art training block equipped with a wide range of facilities.

    • Classrooms with audio-visual systems
    • Group discussion room
    • Conference hall
    • Library
    • Computer lab
    • Forensic science laboratory
    • Wi-Fi

  • Conference Hall

    The Conference Hall of the Academy is in the Training Block.

    It is fully furnished, with digital audio and video equipment, and hosts Academy events.

  • Computer Lab

    The Computer Laboratory has a total of 40 window based and 15 iMacs systems.

    It is used for Basic and In-Service courses and by local children.

  • DFL Lab

    The Academy has a high end Digital Forensic Laboratory with the latest version of cyber forensics software.

    It is used for training courses.

  • Forensic Science Laboratory

    The Forensic Science Laboratory is equipped with the items needed for crime scene investigation, such as blood and drug detection kits.

    About 50 trainees can do practicals at the same time.

  • Library

    NEPA Library has a collection of over 32,700 volumes of books.

    It also has periodicals, magazines, newspapers and précis, and serves trainees, faculty, staff and Academy children.

  • Raag Auditorium

    The Auditorium of the Academy is the focal point of various socio-cultural activities.

    It seats 500, has HD lighting and sound, and also screens films.

  • Small Arms Training Simulator

  • Driving Simulator

    Driving classes are imparted to the Basic Course trainees.

  • Drill Hall

Sports and Fitness

  • Indoor Sports Complex

  • Gymnasium

    The Academy has a state of the art gymnasium, well equipped with aerobic and strength training equipment for trainees and staff.

  • Swimming Pool

    The Academy has a swimming pool, 20 metres long and 13 metres wide.

    It is used for swimming classes in the Basic Course and the Disaster Management ToT.

  • Horse Riding

    The horse riding section imparts basic horse riding training.

    It builds confidence and stamina, and is also a form of recreation.

Health, Welfare and Accommodation

  • Hospital

    The Academy has a 10 bedded hospital headed by a Chief Medical Officer.

    It serves staff, families, trainees and nearby villages, and runs medical camps.

  • Community Hall

  • Shopping Complex

    The shopping complex is centrally located and serves the daily needs of residents and trainees.

  • Messes

    The Academy has various messes to cater to the Basic Course and In-Service Course trainees.

    • Kaziranga Mess
    • Kanchenjunga Mess
    • Cherrapunjee Mess
    • Loktak Mess
    • Aizawl In-Service Mess
    • Bomdila CISF Mess

  • Guest Houses

    The Academy has various guest houses for visiting faculty, resource persons and dignitaries.

    • Nathu La Guest House
    • Kohima Guest House
    • Mawlynnong Guest House

  • Residential Quarters

    The Academy provides residential accommodation to more than 80% of its employees.

    Quarters are of Type I to VI.
