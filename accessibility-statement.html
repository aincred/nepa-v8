Our commitment

We are committed to ensuring that the North Eastern Police Academy (NEPA) website is accessible to all users, irrespective of the device in use, technology, or ability. It has been developed with the aim of providing maximum accessibility and usability to its visitors. As a result, this portal can be viewed on a variety of devices, such as desktop and laptop computers, web-enabled mobile devices, etc.

We have made our best efforts to ensure that all information on this website is easily accessible. We also aim to comply with applicable standards and follow the principles of usability and universal design, which should help all visitors to this portal.

Standards followed

This portal is designed using HTML5 with the objective of meeting the Web Development Guidelines for Websites. It also aims to adhere to Level A of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 laid down by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

Part of the information on this portal is also made available through links to external websites. External websites are maintained by the respective departments, which are responsible for making these sites accessible.

Report an accessibility problem

If you have any problems or suggestions regarding the accessibility of this portal, please contact us to enable us to respond in a helpful manner. Kindly let us know the nature of the problem along with your contact information so that we can take appropriate action.
