Help

Moving around the site

  • Use the main menu at the top of every page. On a phone or small screen, select Menu to open it.
  • Select Skip to main content (the first link when you press the Tab key) to go straight to the page content.
  • Use the breadcrumb at the top of each page to go back to a higher-level page.
  • Footer links give quick access to policies, accessibility, feedback and contact details.

Reading comfortably

  • Use the accessibility button in the top bar to make text larger or smaller, or to switch to high contrast.
  • Use the हिन्दी | English buttons in the top bar to change the language. Your choice is remembered on your device.
  • The scrolling notice and the photo slider on the home page have pause buttons.

Documents

  • Tender documents, recruitment rules, calendars and brochures are PDF files. You can open them in a new tab or download them.
  • To read PDF files you need a PDF reader such as the one built into your browser.
